Same C on x86 and Arm: which is faster?

  • Claude Haiku 5.5 and Sonnet 5.5 wrote fast C for 13 kernels. Every answer was built and timed on an AMD EPYC 9255 (x86) and an NVIDIA Olympus (Arm) sandbox, 2 vCPUs each.
  • Throughput is a tie overall: Arm is faster on 6 of 13 kernels (geometric mean 0.97×). x86 wins wide-SIMD work like matmul; Arm wins CRC-32 by 2.3× with its own CRC instruction.
  • Memory is the same: identical data sizes, and every Arm process peaks about 0.34 MB lower.
  • Told the chip, Claude writes chip-specific code: only 9 of Sonnet's 39 x86 answers compile on Arm.
